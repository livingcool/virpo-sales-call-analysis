# Optimized 100-Point CLOSER Sales Call Rubric & Coaching Guide

## 100-Point Score Distribution
- **Section 1: Preparation & Positioning (10 Pts)** — Research, contextual notes, consultant frame vs transactional seller.
- **Section 2: Clarify & Label (15 Pts)** — Dig beneath surface motives (8 pts); label core problem & get verbal ownership (7 pts).
- **Section 3: Overview Past Pain (15 Pts)** — Run 4-step pain cycle (What tried, How long, When, Outcome) (10 pts); remove guilt while making future their responsibility (5 pts).
- **Section 4: Selling Destination (15 Pts)** — Pitch outcome in < 3 mins (no technobabble) (5 pts); 3-pillar framework mapped to gaps (5 pts); belief-breaking metaphors (5 pts).
- **Section 5: Concerns & Looping (20 Pts)** — Front-load/kill obstacles before price (5 pts); dismantle time/money/spouse distortions (5 pts); loop & re-ask after resolving (5 pts); disarming curiosity/humor (5 pts).
- **Section 6: Reinforce Decision (10 Pts)** — 48-hour remorse prevention (5 pts); continuous closing & onboarding alignment (5 pts).
- **Section 7: Presence & Meta-Skills (15 Pts)** — 2:1 Listen-to-Talk ratio (5 pts); Hot Potato redirect / Smoke frame (5 pts); Absolute conviction (5 pts).

## Hidden Value & Subtext Discovery
- **Unspoken Subtext**: Detect implicit prospect fears, budget hesitations, or unstated motives.
- **Value Opportunities**: Identify exact moments where the rep could have offered tailored reassurance or high-impact solutions.
